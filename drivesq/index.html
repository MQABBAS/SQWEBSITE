Know Before You Start

Your Path To a
Full UK Licence

The official journey from provisional to full licence — and exactly where DriveSQ fits in.

1

Get Your Provisional Licence

Apply online at GOV.UK from age 15 years 9 months. You can start learning and take to the road from age 17.

2

Start Your Lessons

Begin building real skills with a DVSA-approved DriveSQ instructor, on the actual roads you'll be tested on.

3

Pass Your Theory Test

Multiple-choice questions plus a hazard perception test. Use our free 700+ question bank to prepare.

4

Book & Pass Your Practical

We'll advise the best local test centre and timing for your ability, and run a full mock test beforehand.

5

Consider Pass Plus

Sharpen motorway, night and bad-weather driving skills — and potentially lower your insurance costs.

6

Renew Every 10 Years

Your full licence needs renewing well before it expires — the DVLA will remind you when it's due.
