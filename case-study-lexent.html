The Brief

A New Practice That Had to
Earn Trust Immediately

Lexent Law Chamber came to me as a newly established, IAA-regulated immigration and asylum advice practice serving clients across England & Wales. This isn't a driving school or a local shop — it's a regulated legal service dealing with some of the highest-stakes situations a person can face: asylum claims, deportation, detention, family separation.

The brief wasn't just "build a website." It was: build a site that a worried, possibly vulnerable person can land on and immediately trust — one that's transparent about regulation and fees, genuinely useful before they ever pick up the phone, and structured so Google can actually understand and surface it.

Challenge, Approach, Result

Three Problems
I Had to Solve

The Challenge
⚖️

A Trust-Sensitive Market

Immigration advice is a heavily scrutinised sector — the industry regulator has repeatedly flagged issues like hidden fees, unclear regulatory status and slow responses. A new practice needed to visibly stand apart from that reputation from the very first visit.

My Approach
🛡️

Build Compliance Into the Structure

Rather than treating legal pages as an afterthought, I built the full compliance architecture from day one — Privacy Policy, Terms, Cookie Policy, Complaints Procedure, and a visible regulatory badge — plus a page directly addressing sector-wide trust concerns.

The Result
✅

A Site Built to Earn Trust

An 11-page, schema-rich platform live at lexentlawchamber.com — giving the practice a professional, transparent, technically sound foundation to build client trust and organic visibility from launch.

My Process

From First Call
to Going Live

This is the process I followed to take Lexent Law Chamber from a new, unregistered brand to a fully live, structured website.

01
Discovery

Understanding a Regulated Practice

Understanding exactly what a new IAA-regulated immigration and asylum advice practice could offer, its service area, the concerns of the people who'd land on the site, and the compliance obligations that come with giving regulated advice.

02
Sitemap & Compliance Planning

Mapping the Full Architecture

Planned the full site structure: Home, About, Practice Areas, Guides, a Compare page, a Wales-specific page, Contact — plus the mandatory legal suite: Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Cookie Policy, Complaints Procedure.

03
Design

A Tone That Matches the Stakes

Designed a calm, professional navy-and-gold aesthetic with a Playfair Display and Inter type pairing — a deliberately different register from a consumer brand, appropriate for a practice handling sensitive, high-stakes matters.

04
Build

Hand-Coded, Accessible Interactions

Hand-coded the front end in HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript, including an animated typewriter headline that respects prefers-reduced-motion and dynamically locks its height so the page never shifts as phrases change length.

05
Structured Data & SEO

Making Google Understand the Content

Implemented dual JSON-LD structured data — LegalService schema describing services, area served and hours, and FAQPage schema so the site's FAQ answers are eligible to appear directly as rich snippets in Google search results — alongside canonical tags and Open Graph/Twitter Card metadata.

06
Compliance Content

Meeting the Regulatory Bar

Wrote and built the legal disclaimer, complaints procedure, and full policy suite required for a business giving regulated immigration advice, plus a clear, honest statement of the practice's regulatory status.

07
Launch & Conversion Tracking

Going Live — Measurably

Pushed the site live at lexentlawchamber.com with Google Ads conversion tracking wired in from day one, WhatsApp and phone click-to-contact throughout, and a free-consultation booking flow as the primary conversion path.

Built for a Regulated Industry

Trust & Compliance,
Not an Afterthought

Immigration advice is a regulated activity, and the sector's own regulator has repeatedly flagged problems that erode client trust — unclear regulatory status, undisclosed fees, slow responses. I built the site so trust and compliance are structural, not a paragraph bolted onto the footer.

Regulatory Transparency
🔍

Visible, Not Buried

  • IAA-regulated badge shown prominently in the hero, not hidden in small print
  • Clear statement of every area of expertise — asylum, appeals, family/partner visas, work visas, deportation & detention, settlement & citizenship
  • Transparent, upfront fee structure explained in the FAQ before any commitment
  • A plain-English legal disclaimer clarifying no adviser-client relationship is created simply by using the site
Client-Facing Compliance Pages
📋

The Full Policy Suite

  • Privacy Policy
  • Terms of Use
  • Cookie Policy
  • Complaints Procedure
  • Each written, linked from every page footer, and easy to find — exactly what's expected of a regulated professional services site

None of this is legally required for, say, a driving school or a photography site. But for a practice giving regulated immigration and asylum advice, skipping it isn't an option. Building it in from day one meant Lexent could launch fully compliant, not scrambling to add it later.

Search Engine Optimisation

How I Manage
This Business's SEO

SEO for a legal practice isn't just keywords — it's giving Google (and worried, time-pressured searchers) an unambiguous, well-structured signal of exactly what the practice does and who it serves. Here's what's built into Lexent Law Chamber.

On-Page & Structured Data

  • Optimised title tags & meta descriptions on every page
  • Canonical tags to prevent duplicate-content issues
  • Open Graph & Twitter Card tags for clean social sharing
  • LegalService JSON-LD schema (services, area served, hours, price range)
  • FAQPage JSON-LD schema — eligible for FAQ rich snippets in Google search
  • Semantic HTML5 with a clean heading hierarchy

Content, Conversion & Tracking

  • Individual practice-area pages targeting distinct service keywords
  • Dedicated comparison page for trust- and decision-stage search intent
  • Region-specific page (Wales) for geographic search coverage
  • Guides hub for long-tail, informational search queries
  • Google Ads conversion tracking (gtag.js) wired in for paid campaign ROI
  • Clear WhatsApp / phone / consultation calls-to-action on every page
